About

I am a physician-scientist, general surgery research resident, and Clinical Fellow in Surgery at Harvard Medical School. My work focuses on surgical epidemiology, hepato-pancreato-biliary outcomes research, and causal inference, applying advanced epidemiologic and biostatistical methods to pancreatic cancer treatment sequencing, perioperative risk stratification, postoperative readmission, transplantation, health disparities, and surgical education.

My current research program includes risk-stratification tools for patients undergoing pancreatoduodenectomy, a causally informed predischarge readmission-risk model translated into a publicly accessible clinical calculator, and the application of target trial emulation to estimate the effects of alternative treatment strategies for pancreatic malignancies.
